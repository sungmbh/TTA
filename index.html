Our Experts

Core Curriculum & Skill Development

Our curriculum is an intensive, hands-on program designed to transform startups into globally competitive companies. We focus on four critical pillars of success for the US market.

1. Mastering the Pitch

Startups learn to articulate their value proposition instantly. Training covers storytelling, refining the 30-second elevator pitch, building a compelling 3-minute sales pitch, and defining a unique selling proposition (USP).

2. US Market Penetration

We demystify US business culture. Sessions focus on adapting to American expectations, the language of corporate innovation, navigating government funding opportunities (SBIR/STTR), and building strategic partnerships.

3. Digital & Outreach Excellence

Founders are trained to build a powerful first impression online. This includes optimizing LinkedIn profiles, crafting effective cold outreach emails, leveraging Generative AI for sales, and creating a clear and compelling website presence.

4. Live Engagement & Negotiation

The program simulates the high-pressure CES environment. Startups practice booth engagement, lead qualification, party networking tactics, enterprise negotiation, and objection handling to turn conversations into contracts.
